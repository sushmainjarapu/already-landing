Privacy policy

Your practice is yours.

Effective 17 October 2026 · Applies to the Already iOS app and already.toinfy.com

Already is built so that we hold none of your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server copy of your practice. In this version nothing you write, record or score leaves your phone.

The short version

  • Your intent, affirmations, belief scores, plans, journal entries and voice recordings are stored only on your device.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The app has no server and makes no network requests with your content.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising trackers in the app.
  • Delete the app and everything is gone. There is nothing left to request.

What the app stores on your phone

DataWhyWhere
Intent, best-possible-self text, affirmation linesTo run your daily practiceDevice storage only
Belief score, streak, plans, journal entriesTo adapt wording and show progressDevice storage only
Voice recordings of your affirmationsTo play them back during sessionsDevice storage only; never uploaded
Practice times, moon-rhythm settingTo schedule local remindersDevice storage only

What leaves your phone

Nothing. Intent wording, affirmation lines, scene descriptions and sensory cues are produced on the device. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. The app does not use third-party analytics, crash reporting that includes personal content, or advertising identifiers.

Future features

We plan to offer personalised scene images in a later version. If that feature ships, it will be optional, this page will change first, and the app will ask before anything is sent.

Permissions we ask for

  • Microphone: to record your affirmations. Optional; you can read them silently.
  • Notifications: for your morning and night reminders. Optional.

Children

Already is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data. Because no data reaches us, there is nothing to remove on our side; deleting the app removes everything.

Your rights

Because the data lives on your device, you already control it: edit or delete anything inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything. If you are in a region with statutory rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, DPDP Act), you can also write to us at support@toinfy.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Who we are

Already is made by Toinfy. Data-protection contact: support@toinfy.com.

Changes

We will post changes here with a new effective date. Material changes are also shown inside the app before they apply.
