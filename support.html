Support

Most answers are below. Anything else: support@toinfy.com. We reply within two working days.

Getting started

What do I write as my intent?

One sentence about what you are becoming, in the present tense. The app then helps you add a number, a feeling and a date. "I earn more than I need from work I love" becomes "By March 2027 I earn [your number] a month from work I love, and I feel calm about money."

Why does the wording change when I move the belief slider?

Affirmations that feel false can backfire. Below 5 out of 10, Already uses bridge wording such as "I am becoming" or "I choose". As your belief rises, the wording firms up. You can change the score any day from the Today screen.

Voice recordings

How do I re-record a line?

Open You, then Voice, and hold a line to re-record it. Lines also re-record automatically when the wording changes and you confirm.

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your phone. They are never uploaded. If you delete the app, they are deleted too.

Can I skip recording?

Yes. Choose "Read them silently for now" during setup. You can record later from You.

Scenes

Where does my scene come from?

From the best-possible-morning paragraph you wrote in setup. Edit it any time from Visions or You, then tap regenerate for new sensory cues and steps.

Can I put myself in the picture?

Not in this version. Personalised scene images are planned for a later release and will be optional.

Reminders and timing

Reminders are not arriving.

Check Settings, Notifications, Already is allowed, and that Focus modes are not silencing it. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS, so no network is needed.

What is Moon rhythm?

An optional cadence: start a 21-day cycle on the next new moon and check in on the full moon. It is a rhythm some people like, not a mechanism. Turn it off in Practice settings and cycles start the day you choose.

I missed a day. Is my streak gone?

One missed day does not reset the cycle. Two in a row pauses it; pick up where you left off.

Your data

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. All data is on the device, so nothing remains. To clear a single thing, use You, then Data.

Can I move to a new phone?

Already is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). Restore the backup on the new phone and your practice comes with it. There is no account to sign into.

Report a problem

Email support@toinfy.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you tapped before it happened. Screenshots help. We do not need, and prefer not to receive, your recordings.

Accessibility

Already supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels on every control, and Reduce Motion for the breathing ring. If something is hard to use, tell us and we will fix it in the next release.
